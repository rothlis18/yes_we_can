Tokenized transaction barriers involve pay-to-query mechanics, where computational resources are allocated based on token possession. The mathematical relationship can be expressed as:

1. **Query Cost (C)**: The cost of executing a query, proportional to the computational resources required (R) and the token value (V):
   \[
   C = R \times V
   \]

2. **Resource Allocation**: The amount of computational power (P) available per token, which can be modeled as:
   \[
   P = \frac{V}{C}
