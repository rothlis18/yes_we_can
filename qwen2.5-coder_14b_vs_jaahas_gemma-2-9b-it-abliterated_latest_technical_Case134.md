\[ R = \frac{C \times V}{N \times L} \]

Where:
- \( C \) is the computational capacity (in FLOPS) of the edge network.
- \( V \) is the volume of data processed per unit time (in GB/s).
- \( N \) is the number of nodes in the network.
- \( L \) is the latency (in ms) of data transmission between nodes.

\[ R > \frac{C_{\text{min}} \times V_{\text{min}}}{N_{\text{min}} \times L_{\text{min}}} \]

Where:

- \( L_{\text{min}} \) is the minimum latency.

\[ B = \frac{T \times P}{Q} \]

\[ B > \frac{T_{\text{min}} \times P_{\text{min}}}{Q_{\text{min}}} \]
