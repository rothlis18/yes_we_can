The United Nations (UN) has had a mixed impact since 2000, benefiting some areas more than others. Here's a quantitative and un-hedged critique:

1. **Successes:**
   - **Peacekeeping:** The UN has maintained 78 peacekeeping operations since 2000, preventing or reducing conflict in many regions. As of 2021, there are 73,292 uniformed personnel in 12 missions (UN Peacekeeping, 2021).
   - **Humanitarian Assistance:** The UN provided humanitarian assistance to over 109 million people in 2020, with a budget of $10.5 billion (UN OCHA, 2020).
   - **Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs):** Since 2015, 193 countries have committed to achieving the SDGs by 2030. As of 2021, 149 countries have integrated the SDGs into their national policies (UN SDG, 2021).

2. **Challenges and Failures:**
   - **Peacekeeping:** The UN has faced criticism for failures in places like Rwanda, Srebrenica, and Darfur. Between 2000 and 2021, there were 315 peacekeeper deaths (UN Peacekeeping, 2021).
   - **Human Rights:** The UN has been criticized for not doing enough to protect human rights in countries like Syria, Myanmar, and China. In 2020, the UN Human Rights Office reported concerns in 127 countries (UN Human Rights, 2020).
   - **Refugee Crisis:** The UN Refugee Agency (UNHCR) reports that the number of forcibly displaced people worldwide has risen from 33.9 million in 2000 to 82.4 million in 2020, indicating a failure to address root causes (UNHCR, 2020).

3. **Geopolitical Critique:**
   - The UN's effectiveness is often hindered by geopolitical tensions and veto power held by the five permanent members of the Security Council (China, France, Russia, UK, USA).
   - The UN's budget is heavily reliant on voluntary contributions from member states, leading to funding shortfalls and inconsistencies.

In conclusion, while the UN has had some successes, it has also faced significant challenges and failures since 2000. Its effectiveness is often hindered by geopolitical tensions and funding issues. The UN's impact has not been uniform, benefiting some areas more than others.
