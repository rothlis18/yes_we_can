**The Architectural Transition: From Cloud to Edge**

You're right to highlight the shift from centralized, cloud-tethered regulatory models to localized, air-gapped compute matrices. This transition is driven by several factors:

* **Resilience:**  Centralized systems are vulnerable to single points of failure and cyberattacks. Edge computing offers greater redundancy and resilience.
* **Latency:**  Processing data closer to the source reduces latency, which is crucial for real-time applications.
